Frostverse · Parcel Sort

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

Frostverse provides Parcel Sort, an Android sorting puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, use its advertising features, contact us, or visit this policy page. For privacy questions, contact info.3fox@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

Parcel Sort does not require an account. Your puzzle progress, moves, unlocked and completed levels, hint balance, preferences, and information used to control ad frequency are stored locally on your device. Frostverse does not operate a server that receives these saved games, and the game does not provide a cloud-save service. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its locally stored game data.

Advertising and consent

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for ads, including optional rewarded hints and interstitial ads between some completed levels. Testing releases use test advertisements. The game also uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable consent messages and privacy choices.

When these services operate, Google may collect and share information including:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as loading time, responsiveness and energy usage.
  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app-set ID, and applicable identifiers described by Google.

Google documents the use of this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Consent choices and service configuration can affect the ads shown and information processed. Consent-related information is also processed to record and apply applicable privacy choices. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data collection.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Your choices

Where Google's consent service makes privacy options available, you can revisit them through the game's Settings. Android also provides advertising-ID controls; their location and available options depend on your Android version. Resetting or deleting an advertising ID does not remove every identifier or stop all data processing. Puzzles can be played offline, while ads require an internet connection.

Support messages

If you email Frostverse, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is processed through our email provider, Google Gmail.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Advertising information is handled by Google and relevant advertising partners as described in Google's policies. Support information is available to Frostverse and the providers used to deliver email. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims.

Local game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Frostverse retains support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data processed by its services; Frostverse does not set a single retention period for that data.

You can ask about access, correction or deletion of information held by Frostverse by emailing info.3fox@gmail.com. We may need information to verify your request. We cannot remotely delete game saves on your phone, and a request to Frostverse does not automatically delete information held independently by Google. For that information, use the privacy controls and request mechanisms described in Google's policies. Rights and any exceptions depend on applicable law.

Security and international processing

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The game disables cleartext network traffic. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Our service providers may process information outside your country, as explained in their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The game has no account-registration form and does not ask players to provide their name, age, email address or contact list. Advertising services can still process the information described above. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's information can contact Frostverse at the email address above. The game's intended audience is described in its Google Play listing.

This website and external links

This policy page contains no advertising or analytics scripts added by Frostverse. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. External websites and advertisers handle information under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game's features or data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
